# J2ME research coverage

Historical research reviewed November 18, 2026. The underlying catalog snapshot remains October 30, 2026.

- 991 catalog entries, 16,548 reference library records, and 7,427 file inventory entries.
- 59 researched game entries and 35 attributed images or document excerpts.
- The catalog entries outside this research pass retain their source listings and edition data.
- Publisher notices establish what was announced. Reviews cover the versions named by the reviewer. Creator accounts and production images are credited where the work survives.
- Omni Player's ten recorded source inputs retain the filenames and platforms in the project’s game guide. No additional binaries were acquired or compared.
- Some releases appear across Java, BREW, Symbian, DoJa, and N-Gage. Check the platform and edition qualifications on each page.
