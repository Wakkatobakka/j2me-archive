The Witcher: Crimson Trail

Geralt uses two swords and magic signs in a side-scrolling Witcher game for phones.

About the game

Breakpoint’s phone game sends Geralt through twelve stages across four settings, according to the Polish launch notice. The two swords, signs and combination attacks give him several ways through the same fight. The release material also lists three modes: Story, Adventure and Arena.[1][2]

Development

Breakpoint made the Java game under licence from CD Projekt. The phone credits name Paweł Krawczonek for game design, Dominik Wit for programming, Wojtek Ostrycharz and Tomasz Pacyna for art, and Piotr Pacyna for music.[2][3]

Release and reception

Wiedźmin: Krwawy Szlak reached Play subscribers in Poland on October 1, 2007. Play handled Polish distribution for Breakpoint. Hands-On Mobile was named for Western Europe, South America and Asia, with MNI Premium channels announced for Central and Eastern Europe.[1][2]

Samsung prepared a limited L760 engraved with the Witcher logo and loaded with the full phone game, themed ringtones, wallpaper and short clips. The companies used it as a contest prize around the launch.[2]

Russian-language Witcher community coverage from October 15 lists the Nokia N73 among supported phones. Wakka’s input notes name that same model and a Russian copy.[4][5]
