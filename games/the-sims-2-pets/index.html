The Sims 2: Pets

Adopt a puppy, teach it tricks and turn a happier Sim’s pay rises into better treats.

About the game

The Java game opens with a choice of Labrador or Chihuahua. Feed it, take it to the park and teach it tricks. As you complete tasks, your Sim gets happier, earns more at work and can afford better food, toys and accessories.[1]

Development

Petting the dog, teaching it tricks and playing with it all use the same input: match a series of arrows on the directional pad before the timer runs out. The Java review describes an entire routine built around that input, with each task feeding back into the Sim’s ability to buy more for the dog.[1]

Release and reception

EA planned a European phone release for late 2006. O2 advertised the Java game in December under an SMS download code. Pocket Gamer’s Mike Abolins thought the timed inputs and menu of chores made it difficult to feel much attachment to the dog.[2][3][1]

EA Mobile also put out a separate N-Gage edition in June 2008. Nokia’s release had online rankings through N-Gage Arena.[4]
