Orcs & Elves II

A novice thief gets a fantasy RPG built from the low end up after Fountainhead's first Java port forced painful cuts.

About the game

The sequel follows a novice thief trying to save his village. The first dungeon teaches the controls, then a dwarven town gives you people to bargain with, equipment to buy and smaller quests to take on. Those first levels let you spend time developing your character before the heavier dungeon crawl.[1][2]

A mouse named Bob can scout from his own first-person view. If you steal and end up in jail, Bob is one way out. A guard may be frightened of him, while a different cell might call for a lockpick or another prisoner’s help.[2][3]

Development

Katherine Anna Kang outlined a change in approach in 2006. The first game began on high-end BREW hardware and needed substantial work to fit lower-end phones. On the sequel, the team planned to build for the lower-end targets first and add visual detail for more capable handsets.[4]

Release and reception

Electronic Arts announced on December 3, 2007 that the sequel was already available on most North American carriers and was expected in Europe in early 2008. That announcement covers mobile releases together. Pocket Gamer previewed the Java edition on March 27, 2008, and reviewed it on April 8.[1][2][3]
