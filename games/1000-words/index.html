1000 Words

On the way to Olympus, the gods hand you a tablet full of jumbled letters and start the clock.

About the game

Move the cursor across the tablet to build words out of jumbled letters. In one challenge, you have six tries to unscramble a particular word. The gods can hit you with a lightning bolt that hides letters or cuts the time you have left.[1][2]

Quickplay lets you pick timed word-building, a limited-guess six-letter puzzle, or a round that calls for words of particular lengths.[1]

Development

Mathieu Boisvert and Alexandre P. Normand led programming on the Java edition. Alexis Green-Painchaud is credited for both writing and revision, and Mathieu Vachon composed the music.[3]

Release and reception

MobyGames places Gameloft’s release on 19 October 2004, the same date it lists for Babaroga’s BREW port. Gameloft still listed the game in its 2006 catalog.[4][5]

Chris James compared the Java game to the television quiz Countdown in his 2006 Pocket Gamer review. The one control he found awkward was cycling through letters while a timer was running.[2]
