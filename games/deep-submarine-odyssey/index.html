Deep: Submarine Odyssey

Your first submarine can only survive inside a narrow band of ocean. Better equipment opens the depths.

About the game

On a flooded planet, you start out harpooning local sea life from a one-person sub. There is better money in escorting freight and clearing mines. Take jobs at submerged stations and spend the pay on weapons, thrusters and shields.[1]

Pressure keeps a low-end submarine from diving too far, and radiation keeps it away from the surface. Buying a better craft changes which parts of the ocean and which jobs you can reach.[1]

Development

Fishlabs used its ABYSS engine again after Galaxy on Fire. Martin Brüning and Hans-Christian Kühl are credited with the idea, and Kühl programmed the game. The space fighter and its ports gave way to a submarine, diving limits and underwater stations.[2][3]

Release and reception

Mobile Game FAQs lists February 22, 2007 as the release date. Pocket Gamer played a beta that February, then reviewed the game on a Sony Ericsson W810i in March. Sony Ericsson later added Deep to its Fun & Downloads deal with Fishlabs.[4][3][1][5]
