Digital Devil Story: Megami Tensei

Atlus brought the Famicom dungeon crawler to Japanese Java phones in 2004.

About the game

Akemi Nakajima and Yumiko Shirasagi descend into a maze to face Lucifer. They can fight the demons they meet or talk some into joining them. The game began as a 1987 Famicom RPG based on Aya Nishitani’s novel.[1][2]

Wakka records an English J2ME copy under the same title. My Abandonware now lists English and Chinese Java copies. The English translation’s maker and date remain open, and the exact copy Wakka used has yet to be identified.[3][4]

Development

Famitsu’s launch report describes a Vodafone edition with an automap and the option to save anywhere. Atlus also adapted the game for NTT DoCoMo’s FOMA 900i. ITmedia reported that the Japanese DoJa team redrew the graphics and lowered the Famicom game’s difficulty.[2][5][6]

Release and reception

Famitsu reported a December 1, 2004 launch on Vodafone Live! for five Japanese handsets, including the J-SH53. The download cost ¥525. Vodafone used its Java-based V-appli platform on that range of phones.[2][7]

Earlier that year Atlus offered its FOMA version through the MegaTen α portal alongside other games and Aya Nishitani’s revised digital novels. The FOMA free trial added in April capped progression at level ten and disabled saves. A further edition appeared on BBMF’s EZweb BREW service in 2005.[6][8][9]

History sources

  1. Digital Devil Story: Megami Tensei ↗Community bibliography

    Identifies the 1987 Famicom release, Aya Nishitani novel and related mobile releases. Its April 1 date is for the FOMA trial, as period reporting confirms.

  2. Famitsu — Megami Tensei launches on Vodafone Live! ↗2004-12-01 · Archived contemporary launch reporting

    Reports Japanese Vodafone Live release for five handsets, ¥525, automapping and save-anywhere, with game captures.

  3. Omni Player game guide ↗Preservation record

    Wakka identifies an English J2ME input and distinguishes it from the DoJa release. The exact input filename and page are unrecovered.

  4. Digital Devil Story: Megami Tensei — catalogue listing ↗J2ME catalogue

    Supplies one named title page for English and Chinese copies. No binaries accessed or matched to Wakka’s input, and the listing does not settle translation provenance.

  5. FOMA 900i games and Atlus’s Megami Tensei ↗2004-02-05 · Contemporary Japanese trade reporting

    Reports graphics redrawn for mobile, an added automap and lower difficulty for the Atlus FOMA i-appli. Published screenshots are specific to DoJa.

  6. The Famicom Megami Tensei comes to FOMA 900i ↗2004-01-27 · Contemporary Japanese announcement

    Atlus’s plans for the original RPG adaptation within its MegaTen α portal, plus casino games and monthly novels.

  7. Keitai Watch — Vodafone publishes V-appli specifications ↗2003-10-01 · Contemporary carrier technology reporting

    Identifies Vodafone V-appli as Java, including the 256 KB J-SH53 class later supported by Megami Tensei.

  8. Atlus announces a free Megami Tensei trial for FOMA 900i ↗2004-03-31 · Contemporary Japanese launch reporting

    Atlus’s April 1 trial followed the full release. Shows the trial’s limits and ties the launch to author Aya Nishitani’s revised digital novels.

  9. Game Watch — BBMF prepares the EZweb BREW edition ↗2005-09-28 · Contemporary Japanese launch reporting

    Reports BBMF’s separately released EZweb BREW edition and its improvements.
