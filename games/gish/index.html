Gish

Two keys let this ball of tar stick to ceilings or seep through cracks.

About the game

Press * for grip and # to turn runny. You can cling to a ceiling, squeeze past a barrier, or flatten out before jumping to get a little extra height. Coins, fake walls and hidden tunnels give you reasons to return to a level after finding the exit.[1]

Development

Hardwire’s Ondřej Mocný developed the Java version and Erphenic’s Stefan Åhlin made its art. The game credits name Hardwire’s Bloft physics engine. Hardwire showed the Java physics in a trailer when the adaptation was announced in May 2008.[2][3][4]

Hardwire told Pocket Gamer in July 2008 that the game would support the Zeemote JS1, a Bluetooth joystick. The reviewed Java edition also offered separate Bluetooth levels for co-op, deathmatch and races.[5][1]

Release and reception

Pixalon Studios published the mobile game and GlobalFun secured distribution rights in 2008. Kath Brice’s Java review followed in April 2009.[2][6][1]
