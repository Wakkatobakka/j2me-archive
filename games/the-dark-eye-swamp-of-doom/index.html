The Dark Eye: Swamp of Doom

The third Dark Eye phone adventure adapts a tabletop solo story, with encounters resolved through choices and character skills.

About the game

Swamp of Doom was the third installment in The Dark Eye’s Java series. The series let players pick from preset characters and earn points to raise skills they could carry into later adventures.[1][2]

Development

The story comes from Sumpf, a solo adventure for Das Schwarze Auge. Frank Pfeifer is credited for the original text, Stefan Blanck for writing and adaptation, and Rainer Nagel for the English translation. Elkware and Chromatrix worked on the phone series.[3][4][2]

Release and reception

Both MobyGames and the German DSA reference Wiki Aventurica date the Java adventure to 15 July 2003. Golem had reported in May that installments were planned for monthly release through the official site, mobile carriers and phone portals.[1][3][2]

Edition notes

Andresen spoke about the Java series at the launch of its first installment.[2]
