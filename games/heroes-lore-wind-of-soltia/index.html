Heroes Lore: Wind of Soltia

Ronin leaves Basto with a basic sword, and his choice of elemental guardians determines what tricks he learns on the road.

About the game

A trip to the Fire Temple draws Ronin into a search for Reah. The action RPG pairs short and wide weapon attacks with elemental guardians that gain experience alongside the hero. At a blacksmith or refiner you can combine materials and upgrade your equipment. Completing Ronin’s story opens another character’s route.[1][2]

Development

Woongchul Ko led the project and worked on the design. Ryu Hyoung-Seoc wrote the scenario and also designed the game. The credits list a map-tile team separately from character art and illustration.[3]

Release and reception

Game Donga dates the original Korean release of 영웅서기-솔티아의 바람 to December 2005. That edition ran on WIPI. Hands-On Mobile announced the European version through its Best of Korea programme, and Pocket Gamer reviewed the Java game in November 2007.[4][5][6][1]

RPGFan reviewed the English release in October 2008 and found that vague quest directions and sparse explanations put some of the more involved systems out of reach.[2]
