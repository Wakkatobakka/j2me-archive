Galaxy on Fire

Take freelance jobs in the outer reaches of Terran space, then spend the pay on a ship worth keeping.

About the game

You start with a scout ship and a choice of ways to make money. Escort a freighter, join a Terran attack or scoop up cargo for sale back at the carrier. You can sink the money into better weapons and equipment, or trade up to an entirely different ship.[1]

Development

Hans-Christian Kühl took his pitch for an open-world space game to the owners of Fishlabs. The studio started on Sony Ericsson handsets with HI Corporation’s Mascot Capsule engine, then wrote its own middleware to improve how the 3D game looked and played.[2][3]

Release and reception

Gamescom coverage and MobyGames date the first Java edition to 2005. In September 2006, Game Developer described Galaxy on Fire as a release from earlier that year, and Pocket Gamer reviewed the 3D Java game. I-play picked it up the next month, following Fishlabs’ earlier agreement with Arvato Mobile.[4][5][6][1][7]

Pocket Gamer also mentions a 2D edition for handsets that could not run the 3D game. Its review and praise for the graphics apply to the 3D version.[1]
