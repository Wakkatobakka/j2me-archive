Prince of Persia: Warrior Within

Two blades, a stamina meter and a record of your last button presses give Gameloft’s second Sands of Time-era platformer a different rhythm.

About the game

Wall runs drain the Prince’s stamina. He can fight while swinging from chains, and the game teaches a new sword combination after each level. A small display records recent button presses so you can see where a combo went wrong.[1]

Development

The initial production credits name Cristian Soare and Dan Teodorescu as producers and co-designers with Adrian Dorobat and Paul Friciu. A separate Nokia 6280 block adds another production team. The review describes the Series 60 Java edition, where a separate arena puts the sword combinations through fifteen rounds.[2][1]

Release and reception

GameSpot reviewed Gameloft’s mobile game on 1 December 2004. Stephen Palley picked out the extra animation given to traps and the way sound effects cut through brief bursts of music on Series 60 phones.[1][3]
