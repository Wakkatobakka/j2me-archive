Tom Clancy's Splinter Cell

Sam Fisher could wait in a doorway and grab a passing guard in Gameloft’s side-on version of Ubisoft’s stealth game.

About the game

In the side-scrolling mobile game, Sam Fisher can cross overhead gaps, skirt lasers and hide in doorways while guards pass. The rooms are smaller than in the console game, but watching the light and choosing when to come out of cover still matters.[1][2]

Development

The Java credits name Stanislas Dewavrin for production and level design, Nicolas Bouillet and Florent Steiner for code, and Edouard Albert and Stéphane Zettwoog for 2D art.[3]

Release and reception

MobyGames dates the Java edition to 2003. GameSpot’s April 2004 mobile review describes the edition running on a Motorola T720 and singles out the sudden wash of orange when the game moves from the underground missions to the oil rig.[2][1]
