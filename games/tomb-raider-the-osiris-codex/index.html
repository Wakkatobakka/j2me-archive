Tomb Raider: The Osiris Codex

Lara’s first IOMO adventure follows a stolen alchemical text into an Egyptian tomb.

About the game

An anonymous client sends Lara after the Osiris Codex just as the theft of another Egyptian manuscript makes the papers. The caverns give her room to explore and solve puzzles. GameSpot’s Damon Brown took several minutes to get through one early area even when he already knew the way.[1][2]

Development

Glenn Broadway designed the original mobile game and Mike Tucker laid out its levels. The Java credit transcription lists Nick Reed as programmer and three artists: Ben Schroder, Cameron Kerr and Stefan Barnett.[3]

Release and reception

Eidos says The Osiris Codex went on sale through Vodafone live! in the UK on 2 October 2003 and topped that service’s sales chart later that month. Independent figures are unavailable. In November 2004 In-Fusio announced a separate ExEn edition of IOMO’s trilogy, produced by BeTomorrow.[4][5]

Edition notes

The UK carrier date comes from Eidos. MobyGames and GameSpot list different dates within 2003, with no demonstrated link to the UK Vodafone release.[4][6][2]
