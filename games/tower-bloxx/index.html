Tower Bloxx

Stack the floors with one button, then find room for the tower in a growing city.

About the game

A crane swings each floor over the last one. Land the drop accurately and more residents move in. In the city, some towers can go almost anywhere, while the tallest need a mix of lower buildings around them. Good timing gets the tower built. The city grid decides where you can use it.[1]

Development

Sumea’s team started with an idea about a train. The idea changed during brainstorming, and a designer drew a hand dropping a block onto a pile. The team turned that sketch into a throwaway prototype called Drop the Box, then tried paper and Excel versions of the city rules before programming them.[1]

The prototype held people’s attention for about ten minutes. A senior designer and programmer spent three weeks refining how towers sway and floors collide, testing revisions every half hour to three hours. The team drew the city background in code to save space and scale it to different phone screens.[1]

Release and reception

Digital Chocolate published the 2005 Java edition. Tower Bloxx won Best Puzzle Game at that year’s Mobie Awards. Years later, former Digital Chocolate designer Petri Ikonen pointed to its combination of brief sessions and a city that carried your progress forward.[2][1][3]
