Driv3r

Sorrent’s phone adaptation offered two steering schemes for the same stunt-filled chase.

About the game

Undercover driver Tanner follows a car-theft ring from Miami to Nice and Istanbul. The mobile game lets you choose whether the direction pad points the car across the screen or works like an accelerator, brake and steering wheel. The second layout also gives you sharper turns using the handbrake.[1]

Development

Sorrent’s Jill Braff told GameSpot that the company would develop its edition in-house and promote it with Atari. Trade coverage names Robert Nashak as the newly appointed head of Sorrent’s mobile production in May 2004, with DRIV3R among the projects under his remit.[2][3]

Release and reception

Braff described a plan to launch the phone game alongside the bigger versions, with promotion through carriers and Atari’s campaign. MobyGames dates the J2ME edition to 21 June 2004. Reviewing the phone game the next day, Stephen Palley preferred the extra control of relative steering but found the sharp corners and traffic unforgiving.[2][4][1]
