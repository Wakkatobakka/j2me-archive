Tomb Raider: Elixir of Life

Lara tracks her employer to a Scottish castle, where a private museum is guarded by robots.

About the game

With the Codex and cinnabar recovered, Lara goes after the person who commissioned both jobs. The final episode takes her to a billionaire’s Scottish castle, where she has to get past security systems and robots to find out what he’s planning.[1]

Development

Nick Reed, Glenn Broadway, Mike Tucker and Chris Lipscombe are credited again on the finale. Ben Schroder and Stefan Barnett are listed for art and Paul Zimmer for audio.[2]

Release and reception

MobyGames places the original Java edition in October 2004 and the later ExEn edition in June 2005. In-Fusio announced its ExEn licence in November 2004 and credited the port to BeTomorrow.[3][4]
