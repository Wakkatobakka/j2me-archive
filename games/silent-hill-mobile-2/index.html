Silent Hill Mobile 2

A hospital adventure told through hand-painted-looking rooms, a cursor and a shaking crosshair.

About the game

You wake up in a derelict hospital and have to piece together why you are there. The rooms appear as fixed first-person views. Moving the cursor around them reveals objects and puzzles. When you draw a gun, the crosshair shakes and each shot kicks it away from the target.[1]

Development

The game credits name Konami’s European mobile group, Gamefederation Studio and INK Technology. Pocket Gamer’s reviewer praised the logical puzzles and the tension of those short fights. He was less patient with the loading when opening and closing the map, which he reported had carried over from the first mobile game.[2][1]

In the credits, Gamefederation lists programmers and artists across separate roles. With the first-person views held still between steps, the location art carries much of what the player has to pick out. Spanner Spencer’s review points to a trade-off: the scenery helps the tension, while the brief black transitions can make it easy to lose your bearings.[2][1]

Release and reception

Konami showed the Java sequel at Games Convention in August 2008. Silent Hill Memories dates the release to September. Pocket Gamer published a fuller review in November.[3][4][1]
