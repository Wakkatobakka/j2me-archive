Doom II RPG

id Mobile returned to turn-based Doom with three selectable characters and another hostile research station.

About the game

A distress call sends a small squad to a lunar station. You explore the corridors in real time. When you encounter a demon, deciding whether to move, shoot or use a med kit gives the enemy its own turn. Spanner Spencer described the station falling apart almost as soon as the squad arrived. He found the writing had room for jokes in between the firefights.[1]

Development

Jacob Enget worked on a six-person team that developed the game across several mobile platforms in ten months. He says he cut image memory in half in the low-end engine, bringing the total down by a third to fit a 300 KB budget. His résumé identifies that as work on the low-end version and does not assign the figure to every Java handset.[2]

The sequel was among the early projects of id Mobile, the new division led by Fountainhead founder Katherine Anna Kang.[3]

Release and reception

id disclosed the project at QuakeCon in August 2008. Pocket Gamer reviewed a Java build on December 17, 2009. GameFAQs gives more than one 2009 date under its general mobile grouping, so the review is a firmer Java-specific marker than those day-level dates.[4][1][5]
