Asphalt 3 3D: Street Rules

Race recklessly enough and the camera can pull up into the news helicopter following you.

About the game

You can race for first place, wreck opponents in Beat-Em All, or string together stunts in Cash Attack. Upgrades adjust how your car drives and your passenger brings a bonus, such as less police attention or better coverage from the news crew.[1][2]

Development

The team used the 3D version’s moving camera to change the viewpoint during a chase. When a news helicopter turns up, the camera briefly follows the race from above. Gameloft says that new angle meant the cars and scenery needed enough detail to hold up from more than one viewpoint.[1]

Release and reception

Pocket Gamer reported on 22 March 2007 that the 3D edition could be bought directly from Gameloft. The Java release date is separate from the original 2D Street Rules in 2006 and from subsequent BREW and N-Gage service editions.[3][4][1]
