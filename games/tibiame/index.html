TibiaME

Share an online world from a phone with nine inventory slots, one for each number key.

About the game

TibiaME puts players on islands where they fight creatures, look for treasure and solve quests together. Ulrich Schlott described a backpack built to match a phone keypad. Pressing one of the numbers 1–9 jumped straight to its inventory slot.[1]

Development

CipSoft started experimenting on monochrome Java phones. Latency and differences between handsets put limits on the design. Schlott said players used Wi-Fi where they could to save money on data, or bought a monthly pass from a local operator.[2][1]

Release and reception

CipSoft dates the first live service to May 12, 2003, initially on Symbian through T-Mobile. Pocket Gamer’s August 2007 interview places the Java client in August 2006.[2][1]
