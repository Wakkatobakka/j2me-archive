Tomb Raider: Quest for Cinnabar

A commission to recover an ancient vase takes Lara from Egypt to a monastery in Tibet.

About the game

Fresh back from Egypt, Lara is sent to a Tibetan monastery to retrieve a vase of cinnabar. She suspects that the ingredient may be connected to stories about Shangri-La and a valley whose inhabitants live forever.[1]

Development

The surviving credits pair Mike Tucker and Chris Lipscombe on level design, with Nick Reed on code and Glenn Broadway on design. Eidos’s Paul Sheppard is credited as producer.[2]

Release and reception

Eidos expected the episode by the end of 2003, according to an October announcement about the Vodafone release of The Osiris Codex. MobyGames places the Java release in 2004. A separate BeTomorrow edition followed on ExEn.[3][4][5]
