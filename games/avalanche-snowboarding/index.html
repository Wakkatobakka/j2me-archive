Avalanche Snowboarding

Tuck for speed, charge your jump before the lip and use the directional keys for tricks in the air.

About the game

Avalanche Snowboarding turns the descent into an arcade run through rails, cabins and cliffs. Hold the jump key on the approach, release it at the ramp and hit a direction to throw a trick. An especially large jump can be the way over a chasm.[1]

Release and reception

Gameloft Argentina and Gameloft Software Shanghai made the Java release. MobyGames dates it to January 2010, and Pocket Gamer reviewed it on January 29.[2][1]
