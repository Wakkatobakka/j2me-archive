Diamond Rush

The gems are easy to spot. Getting to them without trapping yourself takes more thought.

About the game

A single boulder can block your exit or clear a path through a trap. Red gems can be hidden, and you need enough of them to move on. The trail takes the explorer through Angkor Wat, Bavaria and Tibet, where he can use tools such as a grappling hook to pull obstacles toward him.[1][2]

Development

The Java edition credits Roc Alabern for game design. Gameloft’s Bulgarian and Spanish teams share development credit. Arthur Hugot and Florian Masereel are listed as art directors, with Toni Rodríguez and Doychin Doychev leading the graphic artists.[3][4][5]

Release and reception

Michael French reviewed the Java edition in August 2006. He found that resetting a room cost a life and that the puzzles became more varied as you acquired new tools. Gameloft’s annual reference document also records favorable reviews from Cell Freak and AirGamer.[2][6]
