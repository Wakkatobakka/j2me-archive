Prince of Persia: The Sands of Time

On the Sony Ericsson Z600, learning to wall-run meant learning when to keep the 2 key held down.

About the game

The Prince climbs, fights and works his way through trapped rooms in Gameloft’s side-scrolling version. In the Sony Ericsson Z600 build, the wall-running ability is picked up in the second level. Jump with 2 and keep it held to carry the Prince along a nearby wall.[1]

Development

The Nokia 6101 credit roll distinguishes Ding Xue Song’s original design and production work from Tran Thai Nhan’s work on the handset port. Philippe Arsenault is listed for original sound design.[2]

Release and reception

The J2ME listing dates the game to 2003. Game Over Online reviewed the Java edition in November 2004. The reviewer described the approximately 100 KB download as worth paying attention to if you were on a limited data plan.[3][1]

Edition notes

GameSpot’s July 2004 review mentions a code that unlocks a bonus in the PC game and attributes it specifically to BREW.[4]
